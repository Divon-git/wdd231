NP Membership Benefits

Designed for non-profit organizations.

  • Free chamber membership.
  • Access to community networking events.
  • Non-profit organization directory listing.
  • Access to selected chamber resources.

Ruby Membership Benefits

For businesses looking to establish connections.

  • All NP membership benefits.
  • Business directory listing.
  • Access to selected business networking events.
  • Discounted admission to selected workshops.

Silver Membership Benefits

For growing businesses seeking greater exposure.

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Priority business directory listing.
  • Discounts on chamber events.
  • Access to exclusive training sessions.
  • Business advertising opportunities.

Gold Membership Benefits

Our highest membership level for established businesses.

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Premium business spotlight on the homepage.
  • Priority invitations to exclusive events.
  • Free access to selected training sessions.
  • Premium advertising opportunities.
  • Priority access to chamber networking opportunities.
